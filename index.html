Welcome to Root & Sign

We are a dedicated community movement created by and for Deaf youth and young adults (ages 18–35). Our focus is simple: flat collaboration, real connection, cookouts, game nights, and supporting each other—free from stuffy red tape and ego.

We, not me. Come hang out with us.
